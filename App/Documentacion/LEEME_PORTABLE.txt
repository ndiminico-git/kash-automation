(KASH.App.exe, App y .kash-portable) a tu carpeta habitual.

The package contains no Data, Proyectos, .actualizaciones or .kash files.
Keep those folders and your project files. Do not replace them with an
empty folder. Existing shortcuts to KASH.App.exe continue to work.
El paquete no contiene tus datos. Conservá Data, Proyectos,
.actualizaciones y tus archivos .kash.

LAYOUT / ESTRUCTURA
KASH.App.exe       Launch the app / archivo que abrís.
App/              Required interface files and documentation.
.kash-portable    Keeps app data beside the executable.
Data/             Created or preserved at launch; session and preferences.
Proyectos/        Suggested folder for projects, preserved on update.

Keep App beside the executable. The .NET runtime is included.
Microsoft Edge WebView2 is required, as in the previous release.
Known files from the old flat layout are archived under
App/Archivos_anteriores without deleting their original content.
Unrecognized files and personal folders remain in place.

LANGUAGE / IDIOMA
Choose English or Spanish on the first launch of this version.
Change it anytime in Settings > Appearance > Language, or
Ajustes > Apariencia > Idioma. The choice belongs to this device.
Project names, categories, notes and comments retain your original text.

EXCHANGE ESTIMATOR / ESTIMADOR DE CAMBIO
Hidden by default, including when an older project had it enabled.
Activate in Settings > Visible features > Summary using the private
password. Disable in the same place. Its space collapses when hidden.
The gate is a local app preference, not account-based access control.
